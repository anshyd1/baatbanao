Paise Mangne Ke Message: 15+ Copy-Paste Templates (Har Situation)

Ek page par sab: dost wala, rishtedaar wala, business wala. Copy karo, naam/amount badlo, bhej do.

Dost / close friends ke liye

Bhai, hisaab set karte hain? ₹2,000 tera pending hai, main bhi busy hoon — is Sunday tak bhej de 👇 UPI link
Reminder chala raha hoon bhai 😄 ₹1,500 wala. Is week tak mil jaye toh perfect.
Yo, udhaar wala kaam? ₹3,000 ka. Tu bata ek date, usko main apne khate me fix karta hoon.

Rishtedaar / family ke liye (respect wala)

Bhaiya, aapki ek chhoti madad chahiye — jo ₹2,500 2 mahine pehle diye the, wo is week aap bhej dein toh mera account clear ho jayega. Jald hi bhej dijiye ga.
Didi, hisaab thoda tight hai is mahine. ₹1,500 jo pending hai, kya kal tak aa jayega? Koi tension nahi, bas pooch liya.

Kirayedar / tenant ke liye

Hello, rent ka gentle reminder — ₹8,000 ka July ka payment pending hai. Please 5 tareekh se pehle kar dijiye ga. UPI link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Shukriya!
Namaste ji, rent ka reminder — ₹8,000, due 1st. Payment aaj tak ho jaye toh achcha hoga. Koi problem ho toh bata dijiye.

Tuition / fees (parents ko)

Parents ji, {child} ke tuition fees ka reminder — ₹3,000 (July) pending hai. Please is weekend tak payment kar dijiye ga. UPI link bhej raha hoon 👇
Hello sir/madam, fees ka chhota sa reminder — ₹3,000 pending from 1st. Kripya 7 se pehle clear kar dijiye ga. Thank you!

Customer / client (business)

Namaste, aapka invoice #12 (₹12,500) 15 din purana hai. Please aaj tak payment kar dijiye ga, UPI link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Koi sawaal ho toh bata dijiye.
Hi, gentle reminder — ₹12,500 ka payment pending hai (due 1st). Is week tak ho jaye toh achcha hoga. Link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank
Dear {name}, invoice #12 ab 21 din ka ho gaya hai. Payment aaj tak kar dijiye ga please — warna agla order hold par rahega. UPI link ke saath turant confirm kar denge.

Dukaaan / kirana khata

Bhaiya, tera khata ₹1,850 ho gaya hai. Is Sunday tak set kar lena, phir naya khata khulta hai 😄
Namaste, kirana khata ka hisaab ₹1,850 (12 items) pending hai. Jald se jald payment kar dijiye ga.

Pro tip

Har message me UPI link daalo. 100 me se 40 log "bhai main bhejta hoon" bolke kabhi nahi bhejte — jab tak link unke phone me khula na ho. Link ke saath message = friction zero.

Naam, amount aur tone apne hisaab se daalo

Upar ke ready message se theek thaak ho toh copy karo. Warna 5 second me apna khud ka message banao — language (Hinglish/Hindi/Bhojpuri/English), tone (friendly/firm/funny) aur UPI pay link ke saath.

Aksar poochhe gaye sawaal (FAQ)

Kis language me message dena chahiye?

Jabse insaan baat karta hai, wahi. Dost → Hinglish. Bade rishtedaar → thoda Hindi. Business → English ya Hindi formal. BaatBanao me saari 4 languages hain.

Kitni baar reminder bhejna chahiye?

Rule of 3: Day 1 gentle, Day 4 firm, Day 7 final date. Uske baad ek hi option bacha — settlement ya accept loss.

Message me gaali ya pressure dena chahiye?

Kabhi nahi. 'Savage safe' tone ka matlab hai: strong words, zero gaali. Pressure se insaan defensiveness me jaata hai; clarity se payment aati hai.
