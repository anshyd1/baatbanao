Kirana / Dukaan Udhaar Reminder: Shop Khata Messages (Ready)

AYAnsh Yadav · Creator, BaatBanao|Published 17 Sep 2026 · Updated 28 Sep 2026|3 min read

Dukaandar ka sabse bada dard: udhaar diya, bhool gaya customer. Yeh messages aise hain ki customer sharma ke bhi payment kar de — aur wapas aaye.

Dukaaan udhaar ki 3 cheezein

  1. Khata likh ke rakho (ya BaatBanao ka free Udhaar Khata use karo) — bina hisaab ke message bhejne me sharm aati hai.
  2. Total amount kabhi mat bolo "do-teen hazaar" — exact ₹1,850 bolo. Exact number = serious lagta hai.
  3. UPI link ready rakho — chhote dukaano par bhi UPI ka hisaab ab standard hai.

Regular customer, chhota khata (₹1,000-3,000)

Bhaiya, tera khata ₹1,850 ho gaya hai 😄 Is Sunday tak set kar lena, phir naya hisaab shuru karenge. UPI: upi://pay?pa=your@bank
Namaste ji, kirana ka hisaab ₹1,850 pending hai (12 items). Jald se jald payment kar dijiye ga. Link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank
Bhaiya, chhoti baat — ₹1,850 ka hisaab 2 hafte se pending hai. Kal tak kar dena please, aur agle mahine se thoda cash, thoda udhaar rakhna 😊

Bada khata (₹5,000+)

Bhaiya, tera total khata ₹6,400 ho gaya hai — isme June ka ₹2,200 bhi hai. Is Sunday tak kam se kam ₹3,000 kar dena, baaki 2 week me. UPI link bhej raha hoon.
Namaste, dukaan ka hisaab ₹6,400 pending hai. Please is week ₹3,000 aur agle week ₹3,400 kar dijiye ga. Aise hi udhaar badhta gaya toh dono taraf mushkil hai.

Jo baar-baar late karta hai

Bhaiya, humara rule thoda strict karte hain — ab se 10 din me payment hoti hai warna agla saman cash pe milega. Tera ₹1,850 aaj bhi pending hai, UPI se set kar lena 🙏
Namaste, 3 baar reminder bheja chuka. ₹2,500 abhi bhi pending hai. Please aaj tak payment karein, warna ab se saman sirf cash/online pe hi milega.

Customer bilkul gayab hai (phone nahi uthata)

Namaste ji, kai baar call kiye, nahi mile. Aapka khata ₹4,200 pending hai. Aaj raat 9 baje tak baat nahi hui toh hum ise final consider karenge. Payment link wahi hai: upi://pay?pa=your@bank

Udhaar system ko sehat mand karo

  • "Khata band kar rahe hain" ek baar saal me announce karo — sabse effective reset. 1 week me sab hisaab settle hote hain.
  • Chhote customers ke liye weekly khata day (e.g. har Sunday) — routine = recovery.
  • Udhaar ke liye interest mat bolo, "samman" bolo: "Paisa wapas aane par agla saman 5% off" — positive loop.
Naam, amount aur tone apne hisaab se daalo

Upar ke ready message se theek thaak ho toh copy karo. Warna 5 second me apna khud ka message banao — language (Hinglish/Hindi/Bhojpuri/English), tone (friendly/firm/funny) aur UPI pay link ke saath.

Aksar poochhe gaye sawaal (FAQ)

Dukaaan udhaar me kitna dena chahiye?

Rule: daily turnover ka 10-15% se zyada udhaar mat do. Agar 20 logon ko ₹1,000-2,000 ka udhaar hai toh tumhara cashflow 40,000 band hai — yahi chhota business tootne ki wajah hai.

Khata kahan likhe?

Khatabook app bhi hai, lekin bina app install kiye — BaatBanao ka free Udhaar Khata phone me hi chalta hai, reminder ek tap me bhej deta hai.

Customer payment na kare toh kya karein?

Pehle 3 written reminder (date + amount). Phir likhit final notice. ₹5,000+ ke case me police station me written complaint ka option bhi hai — lekin pehle 2 step se 90% cases solve ho jaate hain.
