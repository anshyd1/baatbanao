भोजपुरी में उधार / पिस मांगने के मेसेज (Ready)

AYAnsh Yadav · Creator, BaatBanao|Published 17 Sep 2026 · Updated 28 Sep 2026|3 min read

Bhojpuri me paise maangna Hinglish se zyada 'apna' lagta hai — aur zyada kaam karta hai. Yeh messages Bhojpuri belt (UP/BIH/JH) ke liye likhe gaye hain.

Dost ke liye (Bhojpuri)

Bhai, chhoti baat kareba — jo ₹2,000 do mahine pehle dekhela, o abhi toh? Is Sunday tak bhej de, UPI link bheja raha hoon 👇
Bhaiya, hisaab khettha kareba? Tera ₹1,500 pending ahe. Is week tak mil jae toh bhalaa hawa.
Arre bhai, udhaar wala kaam? ₹3,000 ka. Tu ek date de, main apna khata theek kareba 😄

Rishtedaar / bade log ke liye

Bhaiya, aapki ek chhoti madad chahiye — jo ₹2,500 aapko dekhela, o is week bhej dein toh mera hisaab balance ho jae ga. Jald bhej dibe ga, UPI id chahiye toh dekhala raha hoon.
Didi, hisaab thoda tight ahe is mahine. ₹1,500 jo pending ahe, kya kal tak aa jae ga? Koi tension nahe, bas pooch liha.

Dukaaan / kirana ke liye

Bhaiya, tera khata ₹1,850 ho gila ahe. Is Sunday tak set karelena, phir naya hisaab khulha jae 😄 UPI link bheja raha hoon.
Namaste, kirana ka hisaab ₹1,850 (12 item) pending ahe. Jald se jald payment karebe ga please.

Funny Bhojpuri (dost ke liye)

Bhai, maine tujhe ₹2,000 dekhela, tu bola 'ek thoda time'. Tin mahine ho gaya — time ab ₹2,000 ka ho gila, time ka kareba? Link 👇
Dosti me sab milha, bas ek cheez na milha — tujhe o udhaar jo 3 mahine se pending ahe! Link bheja raha hoon, ab dekh tera kaam ahe 🪙

Aksar poochhe gaye sawaal (FAQ)

Bhojpuri message dene me koi dikkat nahi?

Bilkul nahi — Bhojpuri belt me yehi sabse natural tone hai. Dost ke liye Hinglish se zyada Bhojpuri kaam karti hai, kyunki 'apni bhasha' me baat hona = serious maana jaana.

Bhojpuri me UPI link bhejna chahiye?

Haan — Bhojpuri belt me bhi UPI ab standard hai. Link bhejne se 'kahan bhejo' ka bahana khatam.

Hindi me bhi option hai?

Haan — BaatBanao me Hinglish, Hindi, Bhojpuri, English chaari bhi hain, ek hi tool me.
