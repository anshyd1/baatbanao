Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Short + seedha, bina legal jargon ke.

Kya hum collect karte hain

  • Khata data (naam, amount, phone, message) browser storage (localStorage) me save hota hai; BaatBanao ke database par upload nahi hota. Jab aap WhatsApp/UPI action use karte hain, selected details destination app ko ja sakti hain. QR banane ke liye encoded UPI payment details third-party QR service ko bheji ja sakti hain.
  • Analytics — page views / basic clicks ka anonymous count (Google Analytics / GA4) collect ho sakta hai. Naam, phone, ya reminder content analytics me nahi jaata.
  • Cookies — analytics aur ads (AdSense) ke liye cookies use ho sakti hain. Aap browser me cookies block kar sakte hain.

Kya hum NAHI karte

  • Aapka data sell nahi karte
  • Khata database hum sell nahi karte. Hosting, analytics, ads, QR, WhatsApp aur UPI providers apni policies ke mutabik technical/payment data process kar sakte hain.
  • Aapke message/phone/amount kisi server pe save nahi karte
  • Children (18-) ka data intentionally collect nahi karte

Third-party services

  • Google AdSense — ads dikhane ke liye, unki apni privacy policy lagti hai.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymous page-view tracking ke liye.
  • Vercel — website hosting (India ke bahar servers, par sirf website files, aapka data nahi).

Aapke rights

Aap kisi bhi waqt apne phone me site clear karke saara data delete kar sakte hain (browser storage clear karo). Koi account nahi hai, kisi server pe kuch nahi hai.
