Client Invoice Follow-up Message: Pending Payment Ke Liye Professional Templates

Client ko baar-baar payment yaad dilana awkward lag sakta hai, lekin cash flow ke liye zaroori hai. Ye templates aise likhe gaye hain ki respectful bhi rahein aur action-oriented bhi.

Best practice: WhatsApp follow-up tab strongest hota hai jab invoice already share ho chuka ho aur aap sirf status ya due-date reminder bhej rahe ho.

Invoice follow-up message formula

  1. Greeting + context
  2. Invoice number / project name
  3. Pending amount
  4. Expected payment timeline
  5. Polite close

Ready-to-copy client invoice reminders

Polite

Hi, a quick follow-up regarding Invoice #204 for ₹18,000. The payment appears to be pending at our end. Please let me know the expected processing timeline. Thanks!

Professional Hinglish

Namaste, Invoice #118 ka ₹11,500 payment abhi pending dikha raha hai. Kripya status share kar dein ya aaj processing karwa dein. Dhanyavaad.

Due-date reminder

Hello, this is a gentle reminder that Invoice #311 for ₹24,000 is due today. Please confirm if payment is scheduled from your side.

After no reply

Hi, following up once again on the pending invoice payment of ₹7,500. Please share an update today so we can keep our records aligned.

Firm but respectful

Hello, this is a final follow-up on Invoice #455 for ₹32,000. Kindly confirm the payment timeline today. If there is any issue from your side, please let us know so we can resolve it quickly.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • “Just checking” type vague message mat bhejo
  • Amount mention kiye bina reminder mat bhejo
  • Emotional ya rude tone avoid karo
  • Payment method ready rakho — UPI / bank details / invoice copy
