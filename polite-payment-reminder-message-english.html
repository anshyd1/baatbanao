Polite payment reminder messages in English

AYAnsh Yadav · Creator, BaatBanao|Published 17 Sep 2026 · Updated 28 Sep 2026|3 min read

A useful reminder is clear about the invoice, amount and next step without sounding accusatory. Choose the stage that matches your situation, then verify every detail before sending.

Privacy tip: Do not put bank statements, full account numbers or confidential client details in a WhatsApp reminder. An invoice number and secure payment method are usually enough.

Before the due date

1. Friendly advance reminder

Hi [Name], a quick reminder that invoice [Number] for ₹[Amount] is due on [Date]. Please let me know if you need the invoice resent. Thank you.

2. Confirming the payment schedule

Hello [Name], I hope you are well. Could you please confirm that payment for invoice [Number] remains scheduled for [Date]? This will help us plan the next stage of work. Thanks.

Payment due today

3. Short and professional

Hi [Name], this is a gentle reminder that ₹[Amount] against invoice [Number] is due today. You may use the payment details mentioned on the invoice. Please share confirmation once processed.

4. Customer-friendly version

Hello [Name], just a friendly reminder about today’s payment of ₹[Amount]. If you have already paid, please ignore this message and accept our thanks. If not, please let us know when we can expect it.

One to seven days overdue

5. First overdue follow-up

Hi [Name], our records show that invoice [Number], due on [Date], is still pending. Could you please check its status and confirm an expected payment date? If there is an issue with the invoice, I will be happy to help.

6. Request a specific date

Hello [Name], following up on the pending amount of ₹[Amount]. Please confirm a specific payment date so we can update our records. A short reply with the expected date would be helpful.

7. Partial payment option

Hi [Name], I understand that schedules can change. If the full amount cannot be processed immediately, please let me know whether a partial payment and a clear date for the balance would be possible.

More than seven days overdue

8. Firm but respectful

Hello [Name], invoice [Number] for ₹[Amount] is now [Days] days overdue. Please arrange payment by [New Date] or reply today with the reason for the delay and a confirmed payment schedule.

9. Work-on-hold notice

Hi [Name], payment for invoice [Number] remains outstanding despite our earlier reminder. Under our agreed terms, further work may need to remain on hold until payment is received. Please confirm the payment status by [Date].

10. Final internal escalation

Hello [Name], this is our final written reminder regarding invoice [Number], due on [Date]. Please pay ₹[Amount] or contact us by [Deadline] with a workable resolution. If we do not hear from you, we will review the next steps available under our agreement.

What makes a reminder effective?

  • Use the recipient’s correct name and invoice number.
  • State the original due date instead of saying “a long time ago”.
  • Ask for one clear action: payment or a confirmed date.
  • Allow for the possibility that payment was already sent.
  • Keep a written record and avoid threats, insults or public shaming.
