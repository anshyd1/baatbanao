Customer Payment Reminder WhatsApp Message (10 Polite + Firm)

AYAnsh Yadav · Creator, BaatBanao|Published 17 Sep 2026 · Updated 28 Sep 2026|3 min read

Payment reminder bhejne se business nahi toot-ta, bhejne se chhodne se toot-ta hai. Yeh sequence follow karo — polite se firm, bina awkwardness ke.

Professional sequence (copy this flow)

DayToneMessage ka matlab
Due -2 dinFriendly"Invoice #12 kal due hai, UPI link bhej raha hoon"
Due +1Gentle"Reminder — payment aaj tak nahi aayi"
Due +5Firm"5 din late, please aaj karo"
Due +10Final"10 din late, agla order hold"

Due -2 din (preventive, sabse important!)

Namaste Amit ji, invoice #12 (₹12,500) kal due hai. UPI link bhej raha hoon taaki aaj hi kar dijiye — upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Koi sawaal ho toh bata dijiye!

Due +1 din (gentle nudge)

Hi Amit ji, chhoti si reminder — invoice #12 (₹12,500) kal due thi. Payment ho gayi toh bata dijiye ga, warna link yahan hai: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Dhanyavaad!
Hello, invoice #12 abhi pending hai (due yesterday). Kya payment bhej di hai? Link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank

Due +5 din (firm, business-like)

Namaste Amit ji, invoice #12 (₹12,500) 5 din overdue hai. Please aaj tak payment karein — link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Koi problem ho toh turant batana, main adjust kar dunga.
Hi, payment ₹12,500 (invoice #12) 5 din late hai. Aaj tak clear karna please. 10 din baad hum agle deliverables pause kar denge.

Due +10+ din (final, consequences clear)

Namaste Amit ji, 3 reminders ke baad bhi invoice #12 (₹12,500) pending hai (10 din overdue). Aaj tak payment na hone par agla work/service hold par rahega. Link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Final baar yahi message jaayega.

Jo "thoda time" bolke phir nahi bhejta

Bhai, samajh gaya — par 'thoda time' ab 3 mahine ho gaya. Invoice #12 (₹12,500) Friday tak milega, warna service hold. Ek hi baar pooch raha hoon, phir expect karunga ✅

2 tips

  • Invoice number + amount hamesha — customer ko "kaunsa payment" ka doubt nahi hona chahiye.
  • UPI link me amount pre-fill karo — 1 tap me payment. BaatBanao ye free karta hai.
Naam, amount aur tone apne hisaab se daalo

Upar ke ready message se theek thaak ho toh copy karo. Warna 5 second me apna khud ka message banao — language (Hinglish/Hindi/Bhojpuri/English), tone (friendly/firm/funny) aur UPI pay link ke saath.

Aksar poochhe gaye sawaal (FAQ)

Payment reminder kitni baar bhejna chahiye?

4 baar max (table dekho). Uske baar baat — call ya meeting. 5+ WhatsApp reminder = customer ko pushy lagta hai, na payment aati hai.

English me dena chahiye ya Hinglish?

Customer ka level dekho. Local business → Hinglish. Corporate/agency → English. Dono me bhi UPI link same kaam karta hai.

Late fee leni chahiye?

Contract me likha ho toh lo. Nahi likha toh pehli baar mat lo — par aage ke invoices me add kar do: '10 din late par 1.5%/month late fee'
