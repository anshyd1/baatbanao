Kirayedar Ko Rent Reminder Message (Hindi + Hinglish, Ready)

Rent maangna business hai, personal nahi — isliye message me feelings nahi, facts hote hain: kitna, kab tak, kahan bhejna hai.

3 rules rent reminder ke liye

  1. Due date se pehle hi bhejo (1-2 din pehle) — jab paisa account me hota hai, payment aasaan hai.
  2. UPI link zaroor daalo — kirayedar ke liye 'kahan bhejo' ka bahana khatam.
  3. Har reminder me amount + month ka naam — "July ka ₹8,000", kabhi sirf "rent" nahi.

Due date se pehle (gentle, har mahine wala)

Namaste ji, ek chhoti si reminder — July ka rent ₹8,000 kal due hai. Kripya aaj hi payment kar dijiye ga. UPI link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank. Dhanyavaad!
Hello, rent reminder — ₹8,000 ka July ka payment, 1st tareekh tak. Jald hi kar dijiye ga toh achcha hoga. Link: upi://pay?pa=your@bank

Due date ke baad (firm, 2-4 din late)

Namaste ji, July ka rent ₹8,000 abhi pending hai (due 1st). Please aaj tak payment kar dijiye ga — 2 din late ho gaya hai. Koi problem ho toh turant batana.
Hello, rent ₹8,000 (July) 4 din overdue hai. Kal tak kar dijiye ga please. Agla reminder final date ke saath jaayega.

1 hafte+ late (final notice tone)

Namaste ji, 3 baar yaad dila chuka hoon — July ka rent ₹8,000, due 1st, ab 9 din late. Please Friday 5 baje tak payment karein. Iske baad hum tenancy agreement ke clauses discuss karenge. UPI link wahi hai.
Hi, final reminder — ₹8,000 (July) 10 din overdue hai. Payment Friday tak nahi aayi toh hum notice process shuru karenge. Koi payment arrangement discuss karna ho toh aaj baat kar lein.

Agar baar-baar late karta hai

  • Ek baar likhit me advance/penalty fix karo: "Ab se 5 din baad ₹200/din late fee" — friendly tone me likho, par likh hi do.
  • Har month ka reminder same time par (e.g. 28 ko) — routine ban jaye toh late aaya toh khud awkward lagega.
  • Payment receipt screenshot me "July rent received ✅" likhwa lo — khata clear, agla dispute nahi.
Naam, amount aur tone apne hisaab se daalo

Aksar poochhe gaye sawaal (FAQ)

Rent reminder kitni baar bhejna chahiye?

28 ko advance reminder, 2 ko due reminder, 5 ko firm, 8 ko final. 15 din baad likhit notice. Is pattern se 80% kirayedar 2nd tak baad me bhej dete hain.

UPI link bhejna professional hai?

Haan — UPI ab officially standard hai. Link bhejna 'card number batao' se zyada professional aur safe hai dono taraf ke liye.

Kirayedar payment nahi kar raha, kya karein?

Pehle 3 likhit reminders (date + amount + due). Phir 7-din written notice. Phir legal route — yahan tak ki kisi bhi baar screenshots save rakho, yehi tumhara record hai.
